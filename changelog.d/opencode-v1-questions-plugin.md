### Added
- OpenCode 1.x questions can be answered in full from the card, multiple-choice and multi-question
  forms included, through an opt-in OpenCode plugin (`integrations/opencode/ovis-questions.js`)
  that takes replies on a private unix socket for its pane. Without the plugin, Ovis reads the
  question off the screen as before. See the guide's OpenCode 1.x questions section.
